Guillermo Toyos-Marfurt

PhD student in distributed computing @ Institut Polytechnique de Paris.

guille@toyos.dev cv dblp scholar github

Hello there! I'm working on the theory of asynchronous, fault-tolerant distributed computing. I study liveness conditions to understand the synchronization necessary to make progress in a distributed execution. In particular, I look at the semantics of the operations we implement. If two operations commute, what do they have to agree on?

I'm doing my doctoral research in the Benagil team, advised by Petr Kuznetsov and Pierre Sutra.
